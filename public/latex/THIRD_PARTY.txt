    pdfTeX and the rest of TeX Live 2026 compiled to WebAssembly by the Siglum
    Project's fork of BusyTeX (https://github.com/SiglumProject/busytex).
    TeX Live's programs are free software under the GNU General Public
    License, version 2 or later, with libraries under the LGPL, MIT and
    similar licenses. If you serve this file from your own site you are
    distributing it: keep this notice, and point to the corresponding source,
    which is that repository at the commit the release was built from together
    with the TeX Live 2026 sources (https://tug.org/texlive/).

releases/<id>/*.data.gz and packages fetched while compiling
    Files from TeX Live 2026: LaTeX packages and fonts, most under the LaTeX
    Project Public License, some under the GPL (cm-super) or the SIL Open Font
    License (Font Awesome and other fonts). Each package's own license is in
    its documentation on CTAN (https://ctan.org/).
